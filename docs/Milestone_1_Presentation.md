## Slide 2: The Problem Statement & Proposed Solution
* **Slide Title:** The Problem: Information Fragmentation in College Hackathons
* **Key Observations:**
  * College hackathon information is scattered across Instagram stories, WhatsApp groups, Google Forms, and Discord servers.
  * Search engines struggle to index essential details like eligibility criteria, team size constraints, exact timelines, and venue logistics.
  * Students experience high search friction and drop off before completing registration.
* **Our Solution:**
  * A centralized, 8-page crawlable static web platform built with semantic HTML5 and responsive CSS.
  * Search-first architecture mapping student search intent directly to dedicated landing pages.
  * Clear educational labeling as an academic mock prototype for Bennett University.
* **Presenter:** Pratik Singh
* **Speaking Notes for Pratik:**
  > "When college students search for hackathon details, they often find broken links or unindexed images on social media. For CampusTech 2026, we structured an end-to-end information architecture that solves this fragmentation by offering dedicated, crawlable pages for each core user intent."

## Slide 6: On-Page SEO Specification & Implementation
* **Slide Title:** On-Page SEO: Headings, Meta Tags & Internal Linking
* **Optimization Standards Enforced:**
  * **Page Titles:** Unique across all 8 pages, strictly under 60 characters with branding (`... | CampusTech Hackathon 2026`).
  * **Meta Descriptions:** Descriptive, compelling calls-to-action under 155 characters.
  * **Heading Structure:** Exactly one `<h1>` per page reflecting primary keyword, structured `<h2>` for subsections, and `<h3>` for cards.
  * **Contextual Internal Linking:** Contextual anchor text connecting Schedule to Eligibility, and Challenges to Registration.
* **Presenter:** Nitin Jhajharia

## Slide 8: Technical SEO: Robots, Sitemap & JSON-LD Schemas
* **Slide Title:** Technical SEO Architecture & Schema.org Implementation
* **Search Engine Assets:**
  * **`robots.txt`:** Fully open crawl policy with explicit pointer to XML Sitemap.
  * **`sitemap.xml`:** Validated sitemap declaring all 8 canonical URLs with crawl priorities (1.0 to 0.7) and daily/weekly change frequencies.
  * **Rich Structured Data (JSON-LD):**
    * `schema.org/Hackathon` & `Event` on `index.html` (dates, address, organizer, free offers).
    * `schema.org/FAQPage` on `faq.html` for expandable Google search rich snippets.
    * `schema.org/BreadcrumbList` on all subpages for structured search snippet URLs.
    * `schema.org/Organization` on footer for entity graph mapping.
* **Presenter:** Dipanshu Singh
* **Speaking Notes for Dipanshu:**
  > "For technical SEO, we implemented comprehensive JSON-LD schemas. Our Event schema defines the physical coordinates at Bennett University and the free admission offer. Our FAQPage schema enables Google to display rich search dropdowns directly in search engine result pages."
