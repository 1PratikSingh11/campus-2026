Innovation Tracks & Judging Criteria

Build high-impact solutions across 5 curated industry tracks. Evaluated by seasoned engineers and founders.

Track 01 🤖

Generative AI & Intelligent Systems

Build AI agents, automated workflow assistants, multimodal reasoning systems, or custom fine-tuned LLM tools that solve tangible problems in education, productivity, or accessibility.

  • Agentic workflows and automated reasoning
  • Multimodal vision & audio assistive apps
  • Local / edge LLM inference for privacy
Track 02 ⛓️

Web3 & Decentralized Applications

Develop decentralized applications (dApps), transparent digital identity verification protocols, peer-to-peer micro-economies, or verifiable credential solutions.

  • Zero-Knowledge identity verification
  • Decentralized content & data governance
  • Smart contract security & auditing tooling
Track 03 💳

FinTech & Financial Inclusion

Innovate around UPI integrations, micro-savings, algorithmic fraud detection, algorithmic credit scoring for underserved demographics, or automated personal budgeting tools.

  • Rural financial literacy & vernacular banking
  • Real-time transaction fraud prevention
  • SME invoice & payment automation
Track 04 🩺

HealthTech & Smart Wellbeing

Create preventative healthcare tools, patient data telemetry, tele-medicine triage systems, mental wellness companions, or hardware-assisted biometric monitors.

  • Emergency response routing & hospital telemetry
  • Mental wellness & student stress monitoring
  • Wearable device telemetry and predictive alerts
Track 05 🌐

Open Campus & Social Innovation

Have an original vision that does not fit standard categories? Build sustainable campus tech, green energy monitors, disaster management software, or open-source developer tooling.

Official Judging Rubric (100 Points Total)

Every project is objectively evaluated by a panel of at least 3 independent judges using the following weighted distribution.

Criterion Weight Evaluation Questions & Assessment Focus
Technical Complexity & Depth 30% Is the codebase well-architected? Did the team implement non-trivial algorithms, APIs, or database schemas? Is the code clean, modular, and performant?
Practical Impact & Utility 25% Does the application solve a real, verifiable problem? How large is the target audience? Does it offer meaningful value over existing solutions?
Innovation & Originality 20% Is the approach novel or innovative? Does the project take a creative angle on an established industry challenge?
Design & User Experience (UX) 15% Is the user interface intuitive, responsive, and accessible? Can a first-time user effortlessly navigate the application without technical guidance?
Live Pitch & Demonstration 10% How effectively did the team demonstrate the working prototype within the 5-minute window? Did they answer technical questions with confidence?
