36-Hour Hackathon Schedule

Stay on track during the sprint. Explore all key milestones from Friday registration to Sunday awards.

Day 1: Friday, November 14, 2026

09:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Participant Check-In & Kit Distribution

Teams verify their college ID cards, collect access badges, hackathon t-shirts, and find their assigned lab bays.

11:30 AM – 01:00 PM

Opening Ceremony & Track Briefing

Inaugural address by academic leadership, introduction of mentors and judges, and in-depth briefing of problem statements.

01:00 PM – 02:00 PM

Networking Lunch & Team Formation

Buffet lunch at campus dining hall. Final team lock-in and desk allocation.

02:00 PM (Sharp)

⚡ Hacking Commences!

The 36-hour countdown officially starts. Initial repository creation and architecture setup.

06:00 PM – 07:30 PM

Workshop: Building Scalable Cloud Backends

Hands-on technical workshop on serverless APIs, containerization, and database optimization.

Day 2: Saturday, November 15, 2026

10:00 AM – 12:30 PM

Mentorship Review Round 1

Industry mentors visit each team's bay to evaluate initial architecture, suggest API improvements, and ensure feasibility.

04:00 PM – 05:00 PM

Afternoon Refreshments & Mini Tech Talk

Coffee, snacks, and a 20-minute lightning talk on AI prompt engineering and vector databases.

08:00 PM – 10:30 PM

Mentorship Review Round 2 (Progress Check)

Mid-way evaluation checkpoint. Mentors review working prototypes and help resolve blockers before code freeze.

11:59 PM (Midnight)

Midnight Pizza & Gaming Break

Recharge with hot pizza, esports gaming lounge, and acoustic musical session.

Day 3: Sunday, November 16, 2026

08:00 AM (Sharp)

🛑 Code Freeze & Repository Submission

All GitHub commits must be pushed. Final README and demo video links must be submitted to the judging portal.

09:30 AM – 01:00 PM

Project Expo & Round 1 Judging

Teams demonstrate their working code at their booths. Judges evaluate according to the 5-criterion rubric.

02:30 PM – 04:30 PM

Grand Finale: Top 10 Stage Pitches

The top 10 finalists present a 5-minute live demo on the main auditorium stage followed by 3-minute Q&A.

05:00 PM – 06:30 PM

Award Ceremony & Closing Valedictory

Announcement of track winners, overall champions, prize distribution, and group photograph.
