Hackathon Registration Portal

Secure your spot at CampusTech 2026. Completely free registration for all eligible college students.

STEP 1

Assemble Your Team

Form a team of 2 to 4 members. Select a unique team name and designate one member as the primary Team Leader.

STEP 2

Pick Your Track

Choose your preferred challenge track: AI/ML, Web3, FinTech, HealthTech, or Open Innovation (you may adjust during Day 1).

STEP 3

Receive Confirmation

After submitting the form, team leaders receive a confirmation email with physical check-in passes and Discord invites.
