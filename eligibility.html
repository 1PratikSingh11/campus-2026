Eligibility Criteria & Official Rules

Clear, transparent rules to ensure fair play, academic integrity, and an inclusive hackathon environment.

🎓

Student Status

Open to all currently enrolled undergraduate students (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc, BCA, etc.) from recognized Indian universities and colleges. Postgraduate students are welcome in open innovation tracks.

👥

Team Size Guidelines

Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Inter-college and inter-branch teams are strictly permitted and encouraged. Solo participation is not permitted to encourage team collaboration.

🪪

Identification Required

All team members must produce a valid physical college identification card at the registration desk upon physical entry on November 14.

Rules of Participation

Rule Area Regulation Details Compliance Action
Originality of Code All software code and assets must be developed during the official 36-hour hacking period. Pre-built applications are strictly forbidden. Commit Verification
Open-Source Libraries Public open-source libraries, frameworks (React, PyTorch, Flutter, etc.), APIs, and foundational models are permitted provided they are declared in the README. Declare in README
Intellectual Property The participating team retains 100% intellectual property ownership of all code, designs, and prototypes produced during the event. 100% Team Owned
Code of Conduct CampusTech maintains zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, or intellectual dishonesty. Violators face immediate disqualification. Zero Tolerance
Hardware & Equipment Participants must bring their own laptops, chargers, extension strips, and development boards (Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ESP32) if working on hardware. BYOD Mandatory

⚠️ Grounds for Disqualification

  • Submitting a repository with commits dated prior to November 14, 2026, 02:00 PM IST without prior disclosure.
  • Plagiarizing another team's solution or unauthorized copying of commercial closed-source software.
  • Breaching campus security regulations or participating under false identity credentials.
