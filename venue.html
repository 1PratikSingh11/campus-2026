Venue Location & Travel Guide

Everything you need to reach Bennett University safely and make the most of your 36-hour stay.

Event Venue Address

📍 Bennett University Engineering Block

Plot No 8-11, TechZone II, Greater Noida,
Uttar Pradesh – 201310, India
Primary Hub: Computing Labs 301–308 & Main Auditorium

How to Reach the Venue

🚇 By Metro

Take the Noida Metro (Aqua Line) to Pari Chowk Station or GNIDA Office Station. Feeder autos and shared cabs are available directly to Bennett University (10–12 minutes).

🚆 By Train

From New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) or Anand Vihar (ANVT), take the Blue Line Metro to Botanical Garden, switch to the Aqua Line, or book a direct app-based cab (approx. 45–60 minutes).

✈️ By Air

From Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL), app cabs (Uber/Ola) take approximately 65–80 minutes via the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway.
